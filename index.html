Decorate mode

Make yourself at home

Choose furniture, then click a free tile to place it. Hold Shift or press Keep placing to place several; Cancel or Escape stops.

Drag furniture to move it. R rotates, Delete removes, Ctrl+D copies, and Escape cancels. Ctrl+Z undoes and Ctrl+Shift+Z redoes.

Right-drag or use the orbit buttons to turn the room all the way round (the wall in front drops low so you can see in); drag up to look down on it, or press Top view under View. Scroll or use the view buttons to zoom. The camera button opens photo mode: hide the controls, frame the room, and save a picture.

Browse furniture, choose an item, then tap a free tile in the room to place it. Keep placing lets you add several; Cancel stops.

Drag furniture to move it. Select an item to rotate, copy, remove, or recolor it in the bottom panel. The arrows in the toolbar undo and redo.

Use two fingers to orbit and pinch to zoom, or use the View buttons. Hide the decorating panel for a bigger view. The camera button opens photo mode to save a picture of your room.

Lo-fi music starts after your first tap or click. Use the music button to turn it off; your choice is remembered.

Save room keeps the open room; Rooms lists every saved design so you can load, rename, duplicate, or delete it. Saves stay in this browser.
